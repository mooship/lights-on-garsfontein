Privacy notice

Last updated 17 October 2026

Garsfontein Grid keeps an open record of power cuts for homes on the Garsfontein substation in Pretoria. It is run by a local resident and is not affiliated with the City of Tshwane or Eskom. This notice explains what the site does with information about you, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Outage confirmations. When you tap “My power’s off too”, we store a scrambled fingerprint of your IP address (a salted SHA-256 hash), along with the outage and the time. It lets us count each connection once per outage. It cannot be turned back into your IP address, and the address itself is never stored.
  • Bot protection. Confirmations are checked by Cloudflare Turnstile, which only loads when you tap the button. Cloudflare looks at signals from your browser to tell people from bots; see Cloudflare’s Turnstile privacy addendum.
  • Request logs. Like most websites, the site is protected by rate limits and keeps short-lived request logs (which include IP addresses) to fix problems and stop abuse. Cloudflare, our host, deletes these logs automatically after a few days.
  • Your theme. If you choose light or dark mode, your browser remembers it locally. It never leaves your device.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts, tracking cookies, analytics or adverts.
  • We don’t sell or share your information.

How long we keep things

Outages and their confirmation counts stay on the site as a public history. If you want your confirmation removed, tell us roughly when you made it; because we only hold the scrambled fingerprint, that is how we can find it.

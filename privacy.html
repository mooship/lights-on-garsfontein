Privaatheids­kennisgewing

Hierdie kennisgewing verduidelik watter inligting oor jou die webwerf hanteer en hoekom, ooreenkomstig die Wet op die Beskerming van Persoonlike Inligting, 4 van 2013 (POPIA).

Wie is verantwoordelik

Die verantwoordelike party is %site.operator.name%, ’n onafhanklike inwoner wat die webwerf in sy persoonlike hoedanigheid bedryf. Daar is geen maatskappy of organisasie agter die webwerf nie.

Wat ons insamel

  • Gemeenskapsbevestigings. Wanneer jy op “My krag is ook af” tik, stoor ons die onderbreking, die tyd en ’n onomkeerbare kode wat van jou internetverbinding afgelei word (’n gesoute SHA-256-hash van jou IP-adres, of vir IPv6 van die /64-voorvoegsel). Die kode sorg dat elke verbinding net een keer per onderbreking getel word. Jou IP-adres self word nie in die databasis gestoor nie. Ná 30 dae word die kode uitgevee; die telling bly behoue.
  • “Steeds af”-verslae. As die webwerf wys dat die krag aan is, maar jy aandui dat jou krag steeds af is, stoor ons die tyd en dieselfde soort onomkeerbare kode, sodat ’n verbinding hoogstens een verslag elke 2 uur kan stuur. Ná 30 dae word die kode uitgevee, en ná 90 dae word die verslag self geskrap. Bevestigings en verslae is tellings van inwoners; hulle verander nooit die status nie.
  • Beskerming teen bots. Bevestigings, verslae en die e-posadres op hierdie bladsy word deur Cloudflare Turnstile beskerm, wat eers laai wanneer jy op die knoppie tik. Turnstile gebruik seine van jou blaaier om mense van outomatiese verkeer te onderskei, en om die kontrole te voltooi stuur die webwerf jou IP-adres saam met die Turnstile-token na Cloudflare. Sien Cloudflare se privaatheidsaanhangsel vir Turnstile (in Engels).
  • Versoeklogs. Cloudflare, wat die webwerf huisves, teken elke versoek aan, insluitend jou IP-adres, die adres van die bladsy, die tyd en jou blaaier se tipe. Ons gebruik dit om probleme op te los en misbruik te voorkom, en tempobeperkings tel versoeke per verbinding. Cloudflare Workers Logs hou hierdie logs vir ’n beperkte tyd wat van die plan afhang, tans tot 7 dae, en skrap hulle dan outomaties.
  • Besoekerstatistiek. Ons gebruik Cloudflare Web Analytics om te sien hoeveel mense die webwerf besoek en watter bladsye hulle bekyk. Dit plaas geen koekies nie en volg jou nie na ander webwerwe nie; sien hoe Cloudflare Web Analytics werk (in Engels).
  • Instellings in jou blaaier. Jou blaaier stoor jou tema (lig of donker), jou taal, watter kennisgewings jy toegemaak het en of jy ’n onderbreking reeds bevestig het. Dit bly op jou toestel en word nie na ons gestuur nie.

Die webwerf het geen gebruikersrekeninge, advertensies of naspoorkoekies nie, en ons verkoop nie jou inligting nie.

Privacy notice

This notice explains what information about you the site handles and why, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Who is responsible

The responsible party is %site.operator.name%, an independent resident who runs the site in a personal capacity. There is no company or organisation behind the site.

What we collect

  • Community confirmations. When you tap “My power is off too”, we store the outage, the time and an irreversible code derived from your internet connection (a salted SHA-256 hash of your IP address, or for IPv6 of its /64 prefix). The code makes sure each connection is counted once per outage. Your IP address itself is not stored in the database. After 30 days the code is erased; the count is kept.
  • “Still off” reports. If the site shows the power as on but you indicate that your power is still off, we store the time and the same kind of irreversible code, so that a connection can send at most one report every 2 hours. After 30 days the code is erased, and after 90 days the report itself is deleted. Confirmations and reports are counts from residents; they never change the status.
  • Bot protection. Confirmations, reports and the email address on this page are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which loads only when you tap the button. Turnstile uses signals from your browser to distinguish people from automated traffic, and to complete the check the site sends your IP address to Cloudflare with the Turnstile token. See Cloudflare’s Turnstile privacy addendum.
  • Request logs. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, logs each request, including your IP address, the page address, the time and your browser type. We use these logs to resolve problems and prevent abuse, and rate limits count requests per connection. Cloudflare Workers Logs keep these logs for a limited period that depends on the plan, currently up to 7 days, and then delete them automatically.
  • Visitor statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit the site and which pages they view. It sets no cookies and does not track you across other sites; see how Cloudflare Web Analytics works.
  • Settings in your browser. Your browser stores your theme (light or dark), your language, which notices you have dismissed and whether you have already confirmed an outage. These stay on your device and are not sent to us.

The site has no user accounts, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell your information.

Purpose and legal basis

We process this information to count confirmations and reports fairly, to run the site and to prevent abuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in doing so (section 11(1)(f) of POPIA). We use it for nothing else.

Who processes the information

Cloudflare, Inc. processes the information on our behalf as operator: it hosts the site and the database, keeps the request logs and provides Turnstile and Web Analytics. Cloudflare’s network is global, so the information may be processed outside South Africa, including in the United States. Cloudflare is bound by its data processing addendum to protect the information and to use it only to provide its services (section 72(1)(a) of POPIA). See also Cloudflare’s privacy policy. We share your information with no one else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • The irreversible code on confirmations and reports: 30 days.
  • “Still off” reports: 90 days.
  • Outages and their confirmation counts: as a public record, without any code.
  • Request logs at Cloudflare: up to 7 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see what information we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. You can also object to the processing. Because we hold only the irreversible code, we can identify a confirmation or report only by its time, and only while the code exists (up to 30 days). After that it is anonymous and can no longer be linked to you. So please tell us approximately when you sent it.

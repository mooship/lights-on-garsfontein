Privacy notice

Last updated 17 October 2026

Garsfontein Grid is a volunteer, open-source project that shows whether the electricity supply in Ward 45, Garsfontein, is on. It is not run by the City of Tshwane or Eskom. This notice explains what information the site processes, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Outage confirmations. When you tap "My power is off too", we store a one-way, salted SHA-256 hash of your IP address together with the outage and the time. The hash lets us count one confirmation per connection per outage. We cannot turn it back into your IP address, and we do not store the IP itself.
  • Bot protection. Confirmations are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which loads only when you tap the button. Cloudflare processes signals from your browser to tell people from bots. See Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy addendum.
  • Rate limiting and security. Your IP address is used briefly, in memory, to limit how often the API can be called. Cloudflare may keep standard request logs to operate and secure the service.
  • Your theme choice. If you pick light or dark mode, the choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no tracking cookies, no analytics and no advertising.
  • We do not sell or share information with anyone.

Retention

Outage records and their confirmation counts are kept as a public history of the local grid. You can ask for a confirmation to be removed; because we only hold a hash, include the date and time you confirmed.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
